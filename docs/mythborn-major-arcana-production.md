# Mythborn Major Arcana production briefs

## Shared master specification

- Master: 1600 × 2800 px, 4:7, no embedded words or numbers.
- Delivery: grid 480 × 840 and detail 960 × 1680 in AVIF and WebP.
- Safe area: keep the primary silhouette inside the central 72% width and 76% height; preserve the full shared frame.
- Palette: midnight navy, deep violet, restrained burgundy and matte warm gold.
- Light: one controlled directional beam plus quiet ambient celestial light.
- Avoid: commercial-deck compositions, Rider–Waite copying, neon, horror, pseudo-writing, random glyph clutter and repeated poses.
- UI: localized card names stay as HTML. Every image uses the localized alt supplied by the manifest renderer.
