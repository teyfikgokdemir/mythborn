# Mythborn Minor Arcana production plan

Minor Arcana production starts only after all 22 Major Arcana masters pass the same-deck review.

## Shared suit direction

| Suit | Visual language | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Wands | Fire, will, motion, sparks, organic dark wood and gold line. | Repeating identical rods or literal flame wallpaper. |
| Cups | Water, emotion, relationship, intuition, moonlight and reflection. | Merely changing the number of identical cups. |
| Swords | Air, mind, decisions, tension, sharp geometry and cold celestial light. | Graphic violence or weapon fetishism. |
| Pentacles | Earth, labour, security, botanical geometry, gold and dark green undertones. | Coin piles or prosperity clichés. |

## Rank composition matrix

Apply every row independently to each suit, producing 56 unique scenes:

| Rank | Narrative requirement | Composition |
|---|---|---|
| Ace | Pure potential of the suit. | One dominant emblem emerging from a spacious elemental field. |
| Two | Choice, polarity or exchange. | Two unequal forces held in a deliberate dynamic balance. |
| Three | First collaboration and visible growth. | Three focal anchors forming an open working triangle. |
| Four | Stability that may become enclosure. | Protected central space with a clear boundary and one opening. |
| Five | Disruption, scarcity or conflict. | Broken rhythm and displaced focal point; preserve visual dignity. |
| Six | Restoration, passage or reciprocity. | Directed movement between two zones with a third mediating light. |
| Seven | Assessment, defence or layered possibility. | Elevated viewpoint with multiple distinct paths or pressures. |
| Eight | Skill, movement or self-limitation. | Repeating rhythm transformed by one decisive human gesture. |
| Nine | Maturity, resilience or near-completion. | Strong solitary figure with accumulated traces of the journey. |
| Ten | Full consequence and transition. | Dense but ordered field showing completion and the next threshold. |
| Page | Curious messenger and early embodiment. | Young timeless figure studies one living suit phenomenon. |
| Knight | Directed momentum with a visible cost. | Diagonal movement; suit element shapes route and resistance. |
| Queen | Inward mastery and generative stewardship. | Stable figure shapes an environment rather than posing with a symbol. |
| King | Outward mastery and accountable structure. | Figure coordinates a wider system; authority without domination. |

## Delivery and naming

- Directories: `minor/wands`, `minor/cups`, `minor/swords`, `minor/pentacles`.
- Names: `ace`, `two` … `ten`, `page`, `knight`, `queen`, `king`.
- Master and derivatives: same 1600 × 2800, 480 × 840 and 960 × 1680 specification as Major Arcana.
- Every final must pass unique-file hashing, decodability, matching AVIF/WebP dimensions, file-size budget, thumbnail legibility and TR/EN/GR alt review.
- Until those gates pass, all 56 entries remain `artStatus: fallback`.
