# Mythborn cinematic visual system

All imagery in this set was generated specifically for Mythborn. No stock photography, hot-linked media, logo replacement, favicon replacement, or third-party visual asset is used.

## Shared direction

- Palette: midnight navy, aubergine, wine burgundy and restrained antique gold.
- Light: narrow atmospheric beams, soft rim light and low-key contrast.
- Materials: dark stone, velvet, brushed brass, embossed paper and selected crystals.
- Guardrails: no people, fortune-teller clichés, horror, neon, readable lettering, watermarks or symbol clutter.
- Crop rule: the primary subject remains inside the central safe area at 390, 768, 820 and 1440 px viewports.
- Delivery: AVIF and WebP responsive variants; social sharing also has a 1200 × 630 JPEG fallback.

## Accessibility

Each in-page visual has natural Turkish, English and Greek alternative text in `public/cinematic.js`. The social image contains no embedded language or text; localized page titles and descriptions remain in the document metadata.
