## Core Symbolic Reading Disciplines (Gözde)

1. **Tarot:** Archetypal card spreads examining past influences, present momentum, and decision crossroads.
2. **Kahve Falı (Coffee Reading):** Traditional Turkish coffee cup symbolism interpreted intuitively around the querent's focus.
3. **Katina:** Traditional mythic symbol readings focused on emotional bonds, intentions, and relationship dynamics.
4. **Duru Görü (Intuitive Insight):** Holistic intuitive reflection addressing underlying energetic knots.
5. **Aşk ve İlişki (Love & Relationships):** Non-judgmental clarity on communication potential, turning points, and shared rhythms.
6. **Yıldızname (Traditional Astrology):** Classical sky lore exploring temperament and life cycles.
7. **Doğum Haritası (Birth Chart):** Real astronomical calculations (Sun, Moon, Ascendant, houses, transits).
8. **Numeroloji (Numerology):** Numerical and letter archetypes of birth dates and names.

## Editorial & Ethical Principles

- **Personal & Bespoke:** Readings are curated one-on-one by Gözde without generic copy-paste templates.
