# Mythborn

> Mythborn es una plataforma educativa multilingüe e independiente sobre Tarot, astrología y simbolismo cultural.

Contenido para educación y reflexión, no pronósticos infalibles ni asesoramiento profesional.

Website: https://mythborn.co/

## Resources
- Tarot card symbolism and cultural guides
- Astrology, birth-chart and current-sky tools where available
- Multilingual educational articles

Mythborn is not represented by an individual tarot reader.
